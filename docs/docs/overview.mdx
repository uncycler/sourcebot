This is a fork of [Sourcebot](https://github.com/sourcebot-dev/sourcebot), a tool that helps you search and understand large codebases.

<CardGroup cols={1}>
    <Card title="Code search" icon="magnifying-glass" href="/docs/features/search/code-search">
        Search and navigate across all your repos and branches.
    </Card>
</CardGroup>

Sourcebot is self-hosted within your infrastructure, and connects to any [code-host platform](/docs/connections/indexing-your-code). Sensitive data never leaves your infrastructure.
